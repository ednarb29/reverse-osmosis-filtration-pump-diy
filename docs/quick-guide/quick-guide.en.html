What the sounds mean

1 short beepShort press recognised
1 long beepLong press recognised
1 very long beepSuper-long press recognised – long flush starting
2 short beepsDouble-tap recognised – manual mode
3 long beepsYour water is ready
1 short + 1 longGreeting – the system has just been switched on
Important: you never have to switch anything off or flush anything by hand. The system ends every cycle cleanly and looks after the membrane by itself. After you stop it with the button the pump keeps running for a few seconds until the pressure is released – do not disconnect anything during that time.

For rare occasions

None of this is needed day to day. Page 1 is enough for making water.

What the system does on its own

Every 8 hoursIt flushes itself so that no germs can develop in the membrane. If it starts up without you touching it, that is normal.
Before filteringIf it has been idle for more than 5 minutes it runs a short pre-flush and discards the first water. The first water after a standstill is poorer – it never reaches your glass.
After filteringAbout 30 seconds after your water is ready it flushes the membrane once more. You can take your water immediately.

Setting the fill quantity (“my bottle is full”)

By default the system filters for 12 minutes. If that does not match your jug or bottle, set the time yourself – no computer needed:

1. StartPut your container underneath and press briefly.
2. WaitWait until the container holds exactly as much water as you want from now on.
3. SaveNow press and hold (1–5 s). The system stops and remembers that duration for every future short press.

The time is kept when the system is switched off (anything from 30 seconds to 1 hour is allowed) and can be set again as often as you like.

Long flush (15 minutes)

Press and hold for 5 seconds until you hear one very long beep. The system then flushes the membrane for 15 minutes. No drinking water comes out; the water goes to the drain.

Useful after a filter or membrane change, after a long standstill (a holiday, say), or if the water ever tastes odd. It always works – even mid-cycle – and a short press cancels it at any time.

Manual mode – for disinfection only

Manual mode exists solely for disinfection with hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂). Quantity, soak time and order are given in the manufacturer's handbook (AquaMichel Mini) – follow the handbook for those. This page only describes what the button does.

EnteringDouble-tap while idle (two quick short presses). You hear two short beeps.
Pump on / offEvery short press toggles the pump: on, off, on, off … This is how you push the quantity of water the handbook asks for through the system.
LeavingPress and hold. The pump stops and everything closes cleanly. The system then behaves normally again.
AutomationWhile you are in manual mode the system does not flush on its own, so it cannot interrupt a long soak.
Caution: in manual mode the membrane does not protect itself – there is no pre-flush and no post-flush. Use it only for disinfection and leave it afterwards with a long press. After disinfecting, flush the system thoroughly as described in the handbook before you drink the water again.
